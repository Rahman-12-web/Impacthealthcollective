About Impact Health Collective

Who We Are

Impact Health Collective is a public health advocacy organization dedicated to promoting health and preventing disease through community campaigns, health education, and awareness programs.

What Inspired Us

Impact Health Collective was inspired by the gaps we saw in health knowledge within communities across Nigeria.

Many preventable illnesses like malaria, hypertension, and cervical cancer continue to affect people simply because they lack accurate information and early intervention.

We believe awareness is the first step to better health outcomes, and we want to create a platform that drives that change at the grassroots level.

The Problem We Address

Low health literacy and limited access to preventive health information can contribute to late diagnosis, poor health-seeking behavior, and a high burden of preventable diseases.

Impact Health Collective aims to address this through health education in communities, schools, and online spaces.

Our Mission

To promote healthier communities through health advocacy, education, and awareness campaigns that empower individuals to make informed health decisions.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every person has the knowledge and resources to live a healthy life and prevent disease before it starts.

Founder & Director

Adekoya Modinat Ajoke

Student of Public Health, University of Ilesha, Osun State

Profile photo — to be added by founder

Biography

"I am a public health student with a strong passion for preventive health and community wellbeing. My journey in public health began with a desire to understand why so many preventable illnesses still affect people in our communities. That curiosity grew into a commitment to use health education, data, and advocacy to drive real change. I believe that knowledge is the first tool for better health, and I am dedicated to making health information accessible to everyone."

Why I Started Impact Health Collective

"I founded Impact Health Collective because I envision a Nigeria where everyone has access to accurate health information and the opportunity to live a healthy life. I saw gaps in health awareness and wanted to create a platform that brings health campaigns, advocacy, and education directly to young people and underserved communities."

Areas of Interest

  • Communicable and Non-Communicable Diseases
  • Epidemiology
  • Health Information, Education & Communication
